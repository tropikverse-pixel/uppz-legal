Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version: Uppz keeps your things on your phone. There is no account, we don’t run servers that store your data, we don’t show ads and we don’t sell or share your data for advertising.

The only outside service Uppz talks to is RevenueCat, which handles Premium subscriptions.

Who we are

Uppz: Daily Quotes (“Uppz”, “the app”) is made by Tropikverse (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at tropikverse@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you set up or create in Uppz is stored only on your phone, in the app’s own storage:

  • Your name (if you give one), goals, moments, topics, tone, look and font choices
  • Your reminder times and days
  • Quotes you’ve seen, liked, saved to Favorites or added to collections
  • Quotes you write in My Own, and any signature or logo image you pick for them
  • Your streak, points, badges and rewards

We can’t see any of this. It isn’t uploaded to us or anyone else. The home-screen widget reads today’s quote from this same storage on your phone.

Subscriptions (RevenueCat, Apple and Google)

If you subscribe to Uppz Premium or restore a purchase, payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

To check whether Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous app user ID created by RevenueCat (not your name or email)
  • Your purchase and subscription history for Uppz (product, dates, status)
  • Basic technical details such as app version, operating system version, store country and your IP address, which every internet request includes

RevenueCat uses this only to provide the subscription service to us. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders and streak nudges are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server. Uppz asks your permission before sending notifications, and you can turn them off any time in Uppz (Profile → Reminders) or in your phone’s settings.

Photos

  • Choosing a logo: when you pick an image for a My Own signature, the app only gets the image you chose. It stays on your phone.
  • Saving quote images: if you save a quote as an image, Uppz asks permission to add it to your photo library. It doesn’t read your other photos.

The background photos in Uppz come with the app. Showing them doesn’t send anything over the internet.

Sharing

When you share a quote, Uppz hands it to the app you choose (for example Messages or Instagram) through your phone’s share menu. What happens next is covered by that app’s own privacy policy.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Uppz currently uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites. If we ever add analytics to improve the app, we’ll update this policy first and say exactly what is collected.

Children

Uppz isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. Since Uppz keeps your information on your device, we don’t hold any personal information about anyone.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Your Uppz data stays on your phone until you delete the app, which removes it all.
  • Cancelling a subscription is done in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel it.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed to provide the service and to meet accounting and legal requirements. To ask for them to be deleted, email us and we’ll handle the request with RevenueCat.

Where data is processed

RevenueCat, Apple and Google may process subscription data in the United States and other countries where they operate, under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top. Important changes will also be noted in the app or in the app’s store listing.
